What is the placement of the nightstands in relation to the king-size bed in the Second bedroom?

A. Both nightstands are placed at the foot of the bed
B. One nightstand is at the corner opposite the door and the other is next to the bed on the far side
C. The nightstands are positioned side by side next to the bed
D. One nightstand is against the wall away from the bed and the other one is on the opposite side

Correct answer: B
